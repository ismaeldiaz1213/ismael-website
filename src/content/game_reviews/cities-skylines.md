## Pre-Game Context 

Before I ever owned a console capable of playing this game, I remember watching [let's plays](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=f7I1q9Rgt3Y&list=PLs6cC73V4fpsQ_ekOFlLb1C8nEzUuJg6g) of people playing SimCity (the 5th one that EA butchered). I remember having a particular investment in watching a city grow from a field of grass to the urban downtown centers that they could become. But SimCity had it's flaws that it never really overcame so when a new game came around called Cities Skylines claimed to be everything fans desired, I was invested in it's success.

The first let's play I had watched was from one of my favorites [OfficialNerdCubed](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4dSaEXBv2C4&pp=ygUabmVyZCBjdWJlZCBjaXRpZXMgc2t5bGluZXM%3D). The game was truly showing what was actually possible and these cities felt far more expansive than could be imagined. And you best believe that for the next couple of years I would be watching youtubers just play this game. Whether it be building a city, fixing traffic (which is a lot more challenging than you'd think), or just goofing around. [Biffa](https://www.youtube.com/@BiffaPlaysCitiesSkylines) is one of my favorites to watch play this game as he was always methodical on his approach to fixing traffic.

All of this background to say, if I ever had to chance to play this game myself, I would take it. And sure enough, when my brother bought a PS4 he also bought a subscription to PlayStationPlus. One of the games you could just play with that subscription was Cities Skylines. And oh boy did I have a blast with the game! I was finally able to implement a city to my liking. Creating highways, transit, and efficient road networks. And I remember when I had my first real traffic issue, I thought, oh boy do I have a fix for this! I'll spare the gameplay details for the gameplay section but I will say, playing the game on console, I quickly saw it's limitations. I bought the game on switch once my brother moved out the house and graphically... it suffered, it was still the same great game, just with similar issues. So at last, once I was able to buy the game on PC, my life felt complete. MODS!!!!!! More on that later though...

## The Hook

I may have gotten a bit ahead of myself but I think the hook here is really the vast open land you see after you pick a map. The simple fact that every inch of space can be built on is truly what makes the game so captivating. You can do whatever you want with this land without having to wait 10 years for an environmental impact report and government interference. And once you start seeing the city grow in ways you couldn't have imagined, you feel this sense of accomplishment. And right away you get the gameplay loop. Build the next area, grow it by zoning, connect it to the rest of the city, plan the next section.


## Gameplay

The road building is generally pretty good. I do have my gripes sometimes about the snapping and some of the "slope too steep" warnings I would often get but I was usually able to terraform my way around it. Transit building is so much fun, the economy can have some challenge at first but becomes pretty easy afterwards, and the game is visually pretty neat though nothing breathtaking.


### Highlights

The mass transit DLC really completes this game. Some of the new transit hubs give you a new ability to plan out the layout of your city. And there's so many ways to be creative with transit which has the added upside of removing most unnecessary vehicle traffic in your city. And it's always so fun to see the hundreds of citizens packing a train station as they wait for the next train. The Industries DLC allows you to really feel like a capitalist as you build many many factories to grow those profit margins. It has it weird bugs but I love it anyway! And there's lots of DLC like this which gives the game that added flow.

Eventually I will screenshot or take pictures of some of my cities and show them here. I think that will speak for the game itself. The fact that anyone would be invested in a game and build for hundreds of hours I think is a testament to how this game can capture your imagination.

### Lowlights

The console versions of the game just don't do it justice. Too often cars would do some silly stuff like stopping in the middle of the highway for no reason. My biggest gripe is always how cars decide to go from the right most lane to the very left lane on a highway. Absolutely no good reason why anyone should do that and on console there is just no way to fix this. That's why if you're on PC, you just NEED the traffic manager mod. It helps counteract some of the goofy AI moves.

And to be honest, there is a point where the repetitiveness of the gameplay loop can feel stale. Once you reach Megalopolis, there's not really much to do. And I can't say I love the idea of obliterating my city just to get some of the unique buildings. Especially the Observatory. I hate having to see so many abandoned buildings. Makes my city look like sections of Downtown Seattle.

Only have a maximum of 9 tiles you can unlock was very likely a performance limitation but I feel as if that should at least be a warning, not a hard cutoff. At least give me the option to burn my computer. Luckily there's a mod for that. And yknow what? More than likely if you have a problem with the game, there's probably a mod for that. 


## Completion & Replayability

Need I say more? I've completed every challenge you can think of and have built various cities beyond Megalopolis. 


## Conclusion

At this point, I see no reason to continue adding more praise to this game. If only Cities Skylines 2 could be just as good. But alas, this game will never die in my eyes and will forever be an 18/17. It deserves the highest honor I can bestow.
